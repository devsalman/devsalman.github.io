Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) emerges as a digital identity paradigm that gives users full control, yet its implementation generally depends on complex blockchain infrastructure. This study aims to design a blockchain-free SSI-based authentication protocol for campus service systems at STT Terpadu Nurul Fikri. The research employs an engineering approach with stages including literature study, architecture design, prototype development, and evaluation ..."
